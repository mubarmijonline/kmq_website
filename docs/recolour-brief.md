# Recolour brief — gold to the logo's blue

**Executed 2026-08-19** — see "The recolour" in `DESIGN-IMPORT.md` for what
shipped. Four things differed from the brief below:

1. `design/admin.css` landed while this was being written and shares
   `tokens.css`. Ten more token references, recoloured with the rest.
2. The list of 21 literals was two short. An inline `rgba(201,168,76,.9)` in
   `blog.html` and a URL-encoded `%23C9A84C` in the select chevron's data URI —
   the second invisible to any search for `#C9A84C`.
3. The hero filters were re-derived rather than left. The brief's `+170deg`
   starting point was right, but a `hue-rotate` alone would have been wrong:
   the same `saturate()` produces a far weaker wash at blue than at gold, so
   both chains needed their saturation re-measured too — `3 → 10` on the
   ceramic layer and `6 → 18` on the tint rim, matched on a canvas against the
   gold each one used to make.
4. `--kmq-blue-deep` is `#1B8FD6`, not the logo's `#0C6BBF`. The brief called
   for the deep blue on borders and gradient tails; measuring it showed the
   only tail it would sit in is the CTA's, where it fails behind a label.

Everything below is measured against the repo as it was before the change:
line numbers, reference counts and contrast ratios are real.

---

## The prompt

Recolour the KMQ website's accent from gold to the blue of the client's logo.

**The site.** A bilingual Arabic/English Flask site for a Saudi car protection
and window tinting company — PPF, nano ceramic, window tint, colour change.
Eight pages plus service and article detail routes, all on a near-black ground.
The visual system was ported from a Claude Design prototype into three real
stylesheets, which are now the source of truth.

**What changes.** The accent only. The five dark surfaces stay exactly as they
are — `#0D0D0D` page, `#0A0A0C` footer, `#111218`, `#16161D`, `#1C1C2E` — and
so does the ink ramp on `#F5F5F0`. What moves is the single warm accent that
currently carries every call to action, eyebrow, hairline, active nav state,
card hover border, section rule, numeral and glow. It becomes blue, taken from
the logo, so the logo stops being the only blue object on a gold page.

### Where colour lives

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `design/tokens.css` | the palette. 10 gold tokens, 4 gold gradients, 1 gold hairline |
| `design/components.css` | ~2100 lines, 94 gold-bearing declarations, **21 of which hardcode gold instead of using a token** |
| `design/base.css` | link colour, link hover, `::selection`, `:focus-visible`, the skip link, the `.kmq-rule` hairline |
| `templates/partials/macros.html:18` | an `icon()` macro with `stroke="#C9A84C"` baked in |

`static/build/` is generated by `scripts/build_assets.py` — never edit it, and
re-run the script after the change.

### The input palette

The logo (`static/img/brand/kmq-logo.svg`) contains exactly three colours: a
shield in a `#0C6BBF → #2EA8E5` vertical gradient, a `#FFFFFF` wordmark, and a
black inner panel. Those two blues are the whole brand input.

### The token mapping

Gold's ramp and its measured contrast on `#0D0D0D`:

| Token | Now | Ratio | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kmq-gold` | `#C9A84C` | 8.50 | resting accent — 39 references, the workhorse |
| `--kmq-gold-bright` | `#D4AF37` | 9.24 | gradient head, focus ring, skip link |
| `--kmq-gold-hi` | `#E4C674` | 11.69 | link hover |
| `--kmq-gold-deep` | `#B4913C` | 6.53 | gradient tail — **0 references**, delete it |

Proposed replacement, chosen so each role keeps its contrast standing:

| New token | Value | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kmq-blue` | `#2EA8E5` | 7.26 | the logo's own light blue. Clears AAA for normal text |
| `--kmq-blue-bright` | `#57BFEE` | 9.34 | matches gold-bright's standing |
| `--kmq-blue-hi` | `#7ACDF5` | 10.99 | hover |
| `--kmq-blue-deep` | `#0C6BBF` | 3.58 | the logo's deep blue. **Borders and gradient tails only — never text, never a glyph.** It fails AA for body copy |

Carry the alpha steps across at the same opacities — `.70 .50 .45 .35 .18` and
the `.07` wash — rebased on `46, 168, 229`.

### The CTA gradient needs care, not a swap

`--kmq-grad-cta` is `linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37, #B4913C)` and the button
label is `var(--kmq-bg)`, i.e. dark ink on gold. That works today: 9.24 at the
head, 6.53 at the tail.

The obvious move is to reuse the logo's own gradient, `#2EA8E5 → #0C6BBF`. Do
not — no single label colour survives it. Dark ink reads 7.26 at the light end
and **3.58** at the dark end; white reads 5.42 at the dark end and **2.68** at
the light end. The gradient is too long.

Narrow it instead: `linear-gradient(135deg, #2EA8E5, #1B8FD6)`, keeping the
dark `var(--kmq-bg)` label. Worst case 5.50:1, passing AA across the whole
sweep. Apply the same reasoning to `--kmq-grad-head`, `--kmq-grad-stat` and
`--kmq-grad-numeral`, which are gold three-stop ramps used as
`background-clip: text` on headings and large numerals.

### Three things that are not colours

1. **The homepage hero tints a photograph with filters, not values.** The
   protection-stack animation paints coatings onto a car cutout with
   `filter: brightness(0) invert(1) sepia(.5) saturate(3)` (ceramic) and
   `sepia(1) saturate(6) hue-rotate(-6deg)` (tint rim) — chains that produce
   gold from a greyscale silhouette with no colour literal anywhere. The rim
   glows at lines 2038–2039 *are* literals and will recolour with everything
   else; these two filter chains will not, and will stay warm against a blue
   page. Re-derive them (a `hue-rotate` near `+170deg` is the starting point,
   but tune it against the render) or say explicitly that you have left them.

2. **`gold` is in the class names.** `.kmq-btn--gold` appears in 8 template
   files; `.kmq-coat--rim-gold` in the homepage hero. Either keep the names and
   accept that they read as historical, or rename and update every template in
   the same change. Do not rename the CSS alone.

3. **The floating WhatsApp button is `#25D366`.** That is WhatsApp's colour,
   not KMQ's. It stays green.

### Constraints

- **Logical properties only.** RTL is a `dir` flip, not a second stylesheet.
  No `padding-left`, `margin-right`, `border-left` and so on — a test in
  `tests/test_site.py` fails the build if a physical side appears.
- **Nothing below 4.5:1** for text on its background, and keep the accent's
  resting contrast at or above 7:1, which is where gold sits now.
- **Both locales.** Every change must be checked at `/ar/` and `/en/`; Arabic
  runs right to left with heavier type.
- **145 tests must still pass**, and `scripts/build_assets.py` must be re-run.

The three edited stylesheets, the macro fix, a note on the hero filters, and a
before/after of the home page, a package card, the warranty seal and the footer
in both locales.
